Working project

“Diaspora Voices in Translation: A Database of 21st-Century Translated Works”

[View the earlier version of the project]

The digital archive and data collection presented above were originally supported by funding from Carnegie Mellon University. With recent funding from Binghamton University’s BUPNUR program, I was able to revise and finalize the database of translated works published between 2001 and 2025, completing the data collection in summer 2026. I am currently developing a more comprehensive version of the website and preparing a scholarly article based on this project. In the meantime, I am sharing this earlier version of the project, which reflects the work I began as a PhD student.
