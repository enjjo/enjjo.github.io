Eunji Jo

Lecturer, Department of Asian and Asian American Studies, Binghamton University

About Research Teaching

Eunji Jo is a scholar of literary and cultural studies whose work centers on diasporic literature and media. She is interested in examining (im)mobility and migration, urban space, queerness and biopolitics in relation to diaspora. She also uses digital and computational methods to trace the circulation of texts and to illuminate broader cultural and historical trends in diaspora studies.
