Critical AI

Description

Artificial intelligence saturates contemporary life. As an idea, it circulates through research, policy, and everyday conversation, and it has become a recurring figure in literature, film, and media. As a technology, it is increasingly woven into how culture gets made: it drafts text, composes music, generates video, and produces advertising. AI is at once a story we tell and a tool we use, and in both forms it has become deeply entangled with the choices we make and the futures we imagine.

Making sense of AI therefore requires social and cultural analysis as much as technical knowledge. What is “artificial intelligence,” and what does it do? How is it imagined? How does it reshape creative and everyday practices? Whose interests do those visions serve? To answer these questions, this course approaches artificial intelligence through the lens of the critical humanities. We will study literature, film, art, and other cultural texts alongside scholarship in critical AI studies.
